Frequently Asked Sourcing Questions

How long does it take to locate a rare or out-of-stock part?

Our automated inventory search scans over 85 verified domestic distributors within 4 hours. Rare imported parts or vintage assemblies typically receive an official price quote within 24 to 48 business hours.

Are sourced parts genuine OEM or aftermarket?

We primarily source genuine OEM components from authorized manufacturer networks. When OEM is discontinued, our logistics specialists locate certified OES (Original Equipment Supplier) alternatives (e.g. Bosch, Valeo, Sachs, Denso) and disclose the brand clearly in your quote.

Am I obligated to purchase the part once a quote is prepared?

No. Submitting a sourcing enquiry and receiving a quotation is 100% free with no purchase obligation. You can accept or decline the quote anytime within its 7-day validity window.
