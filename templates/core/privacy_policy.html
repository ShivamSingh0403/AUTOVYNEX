Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • AUTOVYNEX Platform Data Governance

1. Information Collected

We collect only essential vehicle records (Make, Model, Variant, Registration Number, Chassis VIN) and customer contact coordinates (Phone, Address, Email) to ensure accurate spare part fitment and roadside emergency dispatch coordination.

2. Strict Confidentiality of Sensitive Assets

VIN/Chassis numbers, damaged part photographs, and private garage documents uploaded to AUTOVYNEX are never exposed publicly. They are strictly restricted to the registered owner and authorized automotive sourcing technicians.

3. No Storage of Payment Credentials

AUTOVYNEX does not store debit/credit card numbers, CVV codes, or net banking passwords in any database table. Demonstration payments are simulated within sandboxed test environments.
